Micro-Repair Mini-Guide

Replacing a button: Thread a needle, knot the end, sew through the fabric and button holes 4–6 times, then wrap thread under the button a few times before knotting off.

Fixing a small tear: Use a simple whip stitch along the tear edge, or iron-on patch fabric for a no-sew fix.

Hemming pants: Fold to desired length, pin, then straight-stitch or use fabric tape for a temporary fix.

Local thrift spots near Kambri: Vinnies Dickson, Salvos Watson, Reviver Canberra.
